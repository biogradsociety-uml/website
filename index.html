Who we are

The Biology Graduate Student Society is a campus club that supports all students enrolled in a biology department graduate program. Our goal is to foster a welcoming graduate student community and provide professional as well as social opportunities. While we don't have regular meetings, we do try to hold an event each month to encourage interaction between labs and facilitate interdisciplinary research.
