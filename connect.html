Connect

Slack

This is the best way to stay connected with other biology graduate students in the department! If you want to stay in the loop about BiGSS events, the student union, journal clubs, or more practically, if you're ever looking to borrow a piece of equipment or reagent, then this is the place to be! An invite link should be sent out at the beginning of each semester, but feel free to reach out to a BiGSS eboard member directly and we'll be happy to help you join the conversation sooner!

Email lists

There is a more formal way to contact all the graduate students in the department, and that is by emailing bio_dept_grad_students@listserv.uml.edu ––but be warned, this listserv is not always the most up to date! If you realize you are not getting these emails, the best way to be added is to talk to your PI.

BiGSS EboardAs Biology Graduate Student Society club leaders, feel free to reach out to us with questions about either the department or our graduate student community, and hopefully we can help either answer them or at least point you in the right direction.
