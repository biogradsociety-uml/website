Events

MIT Museum - 11/11/2026

We had a great Veteran's Day visiting the MIT Museum in Cambridge, MA. A group of us took the commuter rail together from Lowell to North Station, and walked over the Longfellow Bridge. We got super lucky with the sunny fall weather! The MIT museum had a fantastic "Sensing Oceans" exhibit that included a crochet coral reef and mesmerizing plankton-inspired glass sculptures. Additionally, we had all had a great time exploring some of their more interactive exhibits. We learned about the potential for keyboard typing tests to diagnose and track the progression of Parkinson's disease. We also wrote lines of poetry back and forth with an AI interface. We were also joined by some members of the GW lab!

Sweet Treat - 10/19/2026

What a great first event! Thanks to all the graduate students who brought sweet treats to share, and also to those who made time in their day to stop by and say hi! It was great to see our friendly community in action. We had plenty to caught each other on, including summer research, fall semester TA schedules, and fun plans for the holiday season. Stefan's homemade lemon squares were a huge hit, as was Nooreen's homemade banana bread! And we can't forget the variety of snacks Austin brought, the sweetest of them being whoopie pies!
